# RB-007: DataNode disk full or near full

## Symptom
A DataNode stops accepting new blocks when its volumes reach the reserved free-space threshold.

## Likely causes
- Data growth outpaced capacity.
- Logs or temp files on the data volume.
- Unbalanced block placement.

## Checks
1. Check volume usage on the node.
2. Compare usage across DataNodes.
3. Look for `No space left on device` in write exceptions.

## Fix
Free space or add volumes, then run the HDFS balancer to even out usage.

## Escalate
Escalate if several nodes are above 90% usage.
