# RB-012: Block scanner and read errors while serving

## Symptom
A DataNode logs `Got exception while serving blk_... to <client>` when a client read fails mid-transfer.

## Likely causes
- The client closed the connection early.
- A network reset.
- A failing disk.

## Checks
1. Check whether the client is a short-lived task that was killed.
2. Look for matching disk errors.

## Fix
Usually client-side and safe to ignore in small numbers. Investigate when rates rise.

## Escalate
Escalate when serving errors rise on one node.
